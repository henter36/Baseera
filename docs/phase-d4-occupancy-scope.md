# Phase D.4 Occupancy Scope

Phase D.4 implements Issue #124 and continues Issue #11 by adding a real Facility Occupancy and Inmate Movement domain. The scope is limited to capacity, census snapshots, movement events, workspace integration, and controlled administrative entry/import.

Included:
- Approved facility and unit capacity baselines.
- Authoritative and internal census snapshots.
- Non-identifying inmate movement events with hashed references.
- Facility Workspace occupancy summary, unit load, movement pulse, interventions, data quality, and timeline events.
- Occupancy management route for capacity, snapshots, and controlled movement import.

Excluded:
- Region and Headquarters Workspaces.
- Broader Resource Center assets beyond capacity.
- PII display, inmate names, or personal identifiers.
- Predictive models, AI, simulation, or optimization.
- Full external inmate-system integration beyond stable import contracts.
